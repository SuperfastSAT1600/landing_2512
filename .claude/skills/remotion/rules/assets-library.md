## License Quick Reference

- **CC0 / Public Domain** — no restrictions, no attribution. Safest.
- **MIT / Apache 2.0 / ISC / BSD** — commercial OK, ship license text.
- **OFL** — fonts: free use, can't sell standalone.
- **CC-BY 4.0** — free commercial, **must credit author**.
- **CC-BY-SA** — credit + share-alike (derivatives same license).
- **CC-BY-NC** — **non-commercial only**. Avoid for monetized YouTube or paid courses.
- **Pexels/Pixabay/Mixkit/Coverr Licenses** — free commercial, no attribution; can't resell asset standalone or use identifiable people in sensitive contexts.

**Monetization rule**: For paid courses or monetized YouTube, treat anything
`-NC` as off-limits. Khan Academy, CK-12, MIT OCW, GeoGebra materials, BBC
SFX archive all have NC clauses — re-create the concept rather than re-use.

## Remotion-Specific Top Picks

- **Fonts**: `@remotion/google-fonts` or Fontsource
- **Icons**: `lucide-react` (small bundle, animatable strokes)
- **Lottie**: `@remotion/lottie`
- **Math**: KaTeX (sync) — see `katex-math.md`
- **Charts**: Recharts or Visx (React-native, drop directly into compositions)
- **Video clips**: Pexels/Mixkit MP4s via `<OffthreadVideo>`
- **SFX**: Freesound CC0 + `<Audio startFrom endAt>`
