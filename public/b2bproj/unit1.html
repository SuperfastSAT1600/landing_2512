incredibly proud —
강한 감정 + 품위를
동시에 표현
＋ 예문 추가
Commitment
"I have come to L.A. to lift trophies and give everything for this club."
트로피를 위해, 이 클럽을 위해 모든 것을 바치러 왔습니다.
give everything for ~ —
헌신을 표현하는
핵심 구문
＋ 예문 추가

Achievement

＋ 예문 추가
Emotion
"It was just amazing."
정말 그냥 놀라웠습니다.
just + 형용사 —

＋ 예문 추가

"I'm so happy to be back."

＋ 예문 추가

Unit 1  ·  Drill

FILL IN
THE BLANKS

01
I'm incredibly _______ to be joining _______.
① proud / excited / honored    ② 팀 이름
예시: I'm incredibly proud to be joining the team.
03
I can't wait to _______ with _______.
① get started / train / play    ② the team / my new teammates
예시: I can't wait to get started with the team.
02
I have come here to _______ and give everything for _______.
① win / improve / prove myself    ② this team / this club
예시: I have come here to improve and give everything for this team.
04
It's _______ to be part of _______.
① incredible / amazing / an honor    ② this club / this team
예시: It's incredible to be part of this club.
