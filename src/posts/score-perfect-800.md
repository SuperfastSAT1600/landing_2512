## Understanding the Digital SAT Math Section

The digital SAT math section is adaptive. This means your performance on the first module determines the difficulty of the second module. To get a top score, you need to crush the first module.

### 1. Master "Desmos" Calculator
The built-in graphing calculator is your best friend. Learn how to solve system of equations, inequalities, and function questions visually.

### 2. Plug in Numbers
For abstract algebra questions, replacing variables with simple numbers (like 2 or 3) can reveal the correct answer instantly.

### 3. Review Your Mistakes
Keep an error log. Every mistake is a learning opportunity. If you don't understand *why* you got it wrong, you will repeat it.

## Conclusion
Consistency is key. Practice 30 minutes every day rather than cramming for 5 hours on Sunday.
